# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be useful to professionals, students, sim racers, and
first-time contributors. Be respectful, specific, and generous with context.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualised attention, doxxing, and
publishing confidential team or personal data are not accepted. Technical
disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behaviour harms the community. To report conduct privately, use the repository’s
[private advisory form](https://github.com/Dabi-init/endurance-stint-planner/security/advisories/new)
and begin the title with `CODE OF CONDUCT`. Reports will be handled as
confidentially as practical.

This policy applies in repository spaces and when representing the project in
public.
